Support and privacy information for the Layerloom graphic design editor on iPhone and iPad.

Get supportPrivacy policy

Support

For questions, bug reports or help with Layerloom, email bawa.inbox@gmail.com. Include your app version, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and the steps that led to the problem. Please do not send passwords or sensitive photos.

Your projects

Projects are saved on your device. Use Settings → Project backup → Export to keep a separate copy. Restore a backup using Settings → Restore a backup → Import. Recently removed projects can be recovered from Settings. Export important work before deleting the app.

Exporting

Open a project and use Export to share PNG, JPEG or SVG files. Save an editable Studio document to continue editing later. SVG font appearance can depend on fonts available in the app used to open it.

Photo tools

Choose an image using Photos or Files. Background removal requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later and a supported image; if processing is unavailable, the original photo remains unchanged.

Privacy policy

Effective 7 November 2026. This policy applies to Layerloom, bundle identifier com.bawwasoft.layerloom, provided by BawwaSoft / Bawantha Rajapaksha.

Your designs stay on your device

Layerloom stores design documents, imported images, project names, previews and preferences locally. Photos are read when you select them through the system picker. Files are read when you choose a file to import. Imported image content is embedded in editable projects and may be included in exported backups. Background removal is processed on your device.

No developer-operated tracking or cloud account

The app does not include advertising, tracking, analytics, developer-operated accounts or cloud synchronisation. We do not receive your designs or photos through the app. Bundled templates and fonts work offline. Apple may separately process diagnostics or device backups according to your settings and Apple's policies.

Sharing and backups

When you export through the system share sheet, you choose the destination. That destination may store or upload the copy under its own privacy terms. Device backups may include app data according to your Apple settings.

Retention and deletion

Removing a project from the gallery makes it recoverable through Recently removed. The app also retains a previous valid library for recovery and may preserve a damaged library file to avoid data loss. Deleting the app removes local app data, but does not remove separately exported copies or existing device backups. Manage those copies and backups separately.

Support correspondence

If you email us, your email address and message are used to respond and resolve your request. Email is handled by the email service providers involved. You may request deletion of support correspondence by contacting us, subject to any retention obligations. We cannot access or delete projects stored only on your device.

This website

This support site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process connection information, such as your IP address, when serving the page under GitHub's privacy statement. This site has no added analytics or advertising scripts.

Changes and contact

We will update this notice if our practices change and revise the effective date. For privacy questions or requests, contact bawa.inbox@gmail.com.
